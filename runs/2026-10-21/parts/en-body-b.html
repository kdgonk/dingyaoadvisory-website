FAQ

When is the registration deadline for a South Africa boarding school?

It varies by South African school, but the rule of thumb is before the end of Grade 6. Hilton College asks for online registration to be complete before the Grade 6 year begins, with a process lasting about 18 months. Michaelhouse closed Grade 8 registration for 2027 entry on 31 January 2026, with later applications going to a waitlist. Work backwards 18 months from the intended entry year and treat that as your own deadline. In Cape Town day schools the first real node is November of Grade 6.

Do applicants need an exam or an interview?

An interview, yes, and some South African schools add an assessment. Michaelhouse bases Grade 8 entry on previous academic performance, a personal interview with the Rector or Deputy Rector, the most recent school report and a confidential recommendation from the current headmaster. Hilton College runs an invitation-only Merit Award Assessment. The interview normally happens during the child's Grade 6 year, on the visit with parents. Bishops in Cape Town adds an entrance exam in English and Mathematics.

Does a child studying in South Africa need a local guardian?

Legal guardianship stays with the parents and does not move because the child boards, but the visa side requires evidence of who is responsible for the child in South Africa plus proof of accommodation. In practice: a parent accompanying the child (whose own permission to stay is a separate matter), a notarised authorisation to a relative or friend, or a school-approved third-party guardianship service. The housemaster manages boarding life, not legal guardianship. In Cape Town, day schools ask for a reachable local adult rather than a housemaster.

What documents does a minor need for a study visa?

Beyond the standard South African study visa pack — acceptance letter, proof of funds, medical scheme cover, medical and X-ray reports, and police clearance for applicants 18 and over staying beyond 90 days — a minor needs an unabridged birth certificate, consent from both parents, and evidence of the responsible adult and accommodation. If a parent is unreachable or deceased, a court order or death certificate replaces that consent. Lodging in Cape Town, the accommodation proof is usually the Southern Suburbs lease.

How early should the study visa be lodged?

September or October of the year before entry into a South African school. The visa must be issued before Term 1 starts in mid-January, and it cannot be lodged without the school's acceptance letter; offers are typically released between February and April of the year before entry, leaving time for notarisation, medicals and any request for further documents. In Cape Town the entrance exam in February of Grade 7 is what unlocks that window.

What does a year at a boarding school cost in total?

Based on the two schools' published 2026 figures, annual tuition plus boarding totals roughly ZAR 417,000 to 441,766 (2026 published rates, not a guarantee of future years). That is only the third layer: add the non-refundable registration and acceptance fees, the refundable foreign pupil's deposit of about one and a half terms, and extras such as books, uniform, insurance and travel.
